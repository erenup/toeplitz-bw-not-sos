\begin{abstract}
Let $X$ and $Y$ be real Toeplitz matrices of order $N$. By the
B\"ottcher--Wenzel inequality, the quartic
$2\|X\|^2\|Y\|^2-2\langle X,Y\rangle^2-\|XY-YX\|^2$ (Frobenius norms) in
the $4N-2$ entries of their symbols is nonnegative. For every integer
$N\ge\ObstructionOrder$ it is not a sum of squares of real quadratic
forms, which disproves a conjecture of L\'aszl\'o (problem MI-15).
The negative theorem is formally verified in Lean~4 with Mathlib. The
obstruction is an exact negative direction of a limiting tangent kernel,
made effective at a finite scale uniformly over all Gram
representations. In contrast, the quartic is a sum of squares for every
$N\le50$.
\end{abstract}
