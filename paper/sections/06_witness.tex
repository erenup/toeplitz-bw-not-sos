\section{An exact negative tangent direction}\label{sec:witness}
For speed pairs with positive real parts define the Hermitian kernel
\begin{equation}
 S_F((a,b),(a',b'))=2\left\{\frac2{A_0^2}+\frac1{C_0^2}
                                      -\frac1{A_0C_0}\right\},
 \qquad
 \begin{aligned}
 A_0&=(a+\bar a')(b+\bar b'),\\
 C_0&=(a+b)(\bar a'+\bar b').
 \end{aligned}                                          \label{eq:tangent-kernel}
\end{equation}
To see the limit directly, substitute
\[
 \xi=i e^{-\epsilon a},\quad \eta=-i e^{-\epsilon b},\quad
 \zeta=\overline{i e^{-\epsilon a'}},\quad
 \omega=\overline{-i e^{-\epsilon b'}}.
\]
Then $A/\epsilon^2\to A_0$, $C/\epsilon^2\to C_0$, and $\Delta{}\to4$.
Consequently $\epsilon^4L_\infty\to S_F$ in \eqref{eq:generating-kernels}: $S_F$ is the
tangent kernel of the corner. Section~\ref{sec:finish} makes this limit
quantitative at the test points.

\begin{lemma}[Exact negative pairing]\label{lem:witness} On the points and coefficients \eqref{eq:witness},
\begin{equation}
                           c^*S_Fc<-2^{42}.             \label{eq:negative-pairing}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof} For \(\ell=(r,j,\sigma)\) and \(h=(r',j',\sigma')\), the
entry denominators are positive integers:
\[
 A_0=(r+r')^2+(\sigma j-\sigma'j')^2,\qquad C_0=4rr'.
\]
Exact integer summation gives the rational enclosure
\begin{equation}\label{eq:witness-enclosure}
\begin{aligned}
 c^*S_Fc&\ge-111807741093765987869670624504700\cdot2^{-64},\\
 c^*S_Fc&\le-111807741093765987869670623456124\cdot2^{-64}<-2^{42}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
To obtain it, round each of the \(1024^2\) weighted entries downward at
denominator \(2^{64}\); the upper endpoint adds one unit per entry, so
the numerators differ by exactly \(1024^2\).
Appendix~\ref{app:witness} specifies the integer algorithm completely and
gives an independent $513$-term regrouping of the same sum.
\end{proof}

The energy identity \eqref{eq:energy} explains each term of $S_F$. For a
single exponential $e_\ell(s,t)=e^{-a_\ell s-b_\ell t}$ one has
$\mathcal H e_\ell=e_\ell/(a_\ell+b_\ell)$, and with $A_0,C_0$ as in \eqref{eq:tangent-kernel}
for the pair $(\ell,h)$,
\begin{equation}
 \int_{\R_+^2}st\,e_\ell\bar e_h\,ds\,dt=\frac1{A_0^2},\quad
 \int_{\R_+^2}\mathcal H e_\ell\,\overline{\mathcal H e_h}\,ds\,dt=\frac1{A_0C_0},\quad
 \frac1{(a_\ell+b_\ell)^2(\bar a_h+\bar b_h)^2}=\frac1{C_0^2}.
                                                         \label{eq:exponential-integrals}
\end{equation}
Summing against $c_\ell c_h$, with the real-coefficient convention of
Section~\ref{sec:mech-tangent}, gives \eqref{eq:energy} term by term.
The signed pairs in \eqref{eq:witness} make $\phi$ antisymmetric and
cancel the middle term. Their exponential difference is
$-2i e^{-r(s+t)}\sin(j(s-t))$; its squared factor explains the four in
\eqref{eq:witness-convolution}. The negative channel in \eqref{eq:energy} is a
diagonal-tail Hardy energy. Removing it makes the exact pairing positive.
