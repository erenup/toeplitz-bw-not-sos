\section{The exact integer witness}\label{app:witness}
This appendix specifies the rational sign check of
Lemma~\ref{lem:witness} completely. No approximation of a transcendental
number enters the check: for $\ell=(r,j,\sigma)$ and $h=(r',j',\sigma')$
the complex speeds only lead to the positive integers
\[
 A_0=(r+r')^2+(\sigma j-\sigma'j')^2,\qquad C_0=4rr'.
\]
On these points,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:entry-rational}
 (S_F)_{\ell h}
       =\frac{4C_0^2+2A_0^2-2A_0C_0}{A_0^2C_0^2}.
\end{equation}
The integer weights $\hat c_j$ are given by the floor formula \eqref{eq:witness}.
Over the $1024^2$ ordered pairs compute
\begin{equation}\label{eq:integer-enclosure}
 J=\sum_{\ell,h}\left\lfloor\frac{2^{64}c_\ell c_h(4C_0^2+2A_0^2-2A_0C_0)}
 {A_0^2C_0^2}\right\rfloor,
\end{equation}
with every quotient rounded toward negative infinity. Each summand
bounds its exact rational entry from below with error less than one
unit at denominator $2^{64}$. Consequently
\[
 \frac{J}{2^{64}}\le c^*S_Fc\le\frac{J+1048576}{2^{64}}.
\]
The exact outputs are
\begin{align*}
 J&=-111807741093765987869670624504700,\\
 J+1048576&=-111807741093765987869670623456124,\\
 \|c\|_1&=859652168.
\end{align*}
The first two are the numerators of the endpoints in \eqref{eq:witness-enclosure}.
The upper numerator is
smaller than $-2^{106}$, proving $c^*S_Fc<-2^{42}$. All loops have fixed
finite bounds; the formulas above are a full specification of the
calculation.

\subsection{A regrouped sum}
Grouping the four sign choices gives a shorter exact summation. Define
\[
 d_l=\sum_{j,j'=1}^{256}\hat c_j\hat c_{j'}
       (\one_{|j-j'|=l}-\one_{j+j'=l}),\qquad0\le l\le512,
\]
and
\[
 \mathcal K(l)=\sum_{r,r'\in\{1,2\}}
 \left(\frac2{((r+r')^2+l^2)^2}
       -\frac1{4rr'((r+r')^2+l^2)}\right).
\]
Expanding the four sign choices in \eqref{eq:entry-rational} cancels the
rank-one term, because $\sum_{\sigma,\sigma'}\sigma\sigma'=0$, and gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:witness-convolution}
                        c^*S_Fc=4\sum_{l=0}^{512}d_l\mathcal K(l).
\end{equation}
This is a 513-term exact rational sum after integer convolution of the
weights, and it agrees with the full-entry enclosure. The factor four
comes from replacing a difference of two exponentials by a sine.

\subsection{Normalization}
If the negative contribution $-2/(A_0C_0)$ is removed from every entry,
the pairing becomes strictly positive; the accompanying checker verifies
this, the enclosure above, and that the convolution
\eqref{eq:witness-convolution} lies in it, all in exact arithmetic. Relative to the coefficient mass, the
defect $\delta_{\rm test}=-c^*S_Fc/\|c\|_1^2$ satisfies
\[
                            2^{-18}<\delta_{\rm test}<2^{-16}
\]
by the same endpoints. This interval describes this particular test,
not the smallest eigenvalue of $S_F$ or an optimal sum-of-squares
budget.
